# Code of conduct

Ghostrol is made by people who play games in their spare time. Keep it a place where anyone can ask a question, report a bug or send a fix.

## Expected

- Be patient and kind, especially with people new to GitHub or to coding.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and why.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or slurs of any kind.
- Sexual language or images.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Spam and advertising.
- Asking for help with cheating in online games. Ghostrol is for playing with a keyboard and mouse, not for getting around a game's rules.

## What happens

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules. To report something, use the Report link on the comment or write to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are kept private.

This applies to the repository, its issues and discussions, and anywhere someone speaks for the project.
