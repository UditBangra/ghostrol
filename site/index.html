Free, start to finish

Moves like a stick

Keys can ease in, so you can walk and sneak. Mouse aim has speed, curve and smoothing you can tune.

Ready for 3,600+ games

Every cloud game gets a layout for its genre, popular games get tuned presets, and you can make your own.

Knows when to step aside

Games with their own mouse and keyboard support, like Fortnite, are left alone.

Sends nothing

No analytics, no network requests. Your keys stay in your browser.

Controls over the game

Hold Alt+C to see your keys mid-race, or tap it to switch presets, even in full screen.
